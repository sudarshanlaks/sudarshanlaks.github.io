What the data shows (all categories, September 2026)

  • Stock is worth EUR 2.90M and 47% of it, EUR 1.36M, is safety stock.
  • No SKU fell below its safety stock in 33 months (1,320 SKU-months). The closest any SKU came was 1.13 times its safety stock, and the typical SKU never went below 1.8 times.
  • The unused buffer adds up to EUR 0.89M. Home Care holds the most (EUR 0.38M), then Personal Care (EUR 0.29M).
  • 19 SKUs hold more than safety stock plus one month of demand, worth EUR 0.10M. 13 of them are C-class, which hold 64 days of cover against 46 for B and 55 for A.
  • No SKU is projected below safety stock in October to December 2026.

Recommendation

Lower safety stock in steps, starting with Home Care and Personal Care. Releasing half of the unused buffer would free about EUR 0.44M. Then slow replenishment for the 13 C-class SKUs in excess. The buffer is judged on 33 months of history only, so review it after each step.

Days of cover = closing stock / monthly demand x 30. Excess = stock above safety stock plus one month of demand. Inventory value = units x unit cost. Safety stock share = safety stock value / inventory value.
