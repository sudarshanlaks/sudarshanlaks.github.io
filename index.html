  • Forecast accuracy

    How accurate are the statistical and planner forecasts, and does the planner's adjustment help? Measured with WAPE, bias and forecast value add across 40 SKUs and 3 channels.

  • Forecasting models

    Can a simple method beat the current statistical forecast? Five methods tested one month ahead over 21 months and compared by category.
