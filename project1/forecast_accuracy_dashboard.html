What the data shows (all channels, all categories)

  • The planner adjustment adds no value overall: consensus WAPE is 16.2% against 15.8% for the statistical forecast.
  • Food is over-forecast by 4.9% and the adjustment makes it 1.5 points worse.
  • Beverages are under-forecast by 8.0% and have the highest error (20.6%), driven by summer peaks.
  • E-commerce is the hardest channel: 18.5% error and 7.7% under-forecast.

Recommendation

Stop the manual adjustment on Food and Beverages unless the planner has new information, such as a promotion or a listing change. Review the Beverages seasonal profile, the largest source of under-forecasting.

WAPE = sum of absolute errors / sum of actual sales. Bias = sum of (forecast - actual) / sum of actual. Forecast value add = statistical WAPE - consensus WAPE; above zero means the adjustment helped. Forecasts are made one month ahead.
