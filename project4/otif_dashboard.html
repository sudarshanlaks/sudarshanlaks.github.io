What the data shows (all suppliers, all periods)

  • OTIF is 86.2% against a target of 95%, a gap of 8.8 points. On time is 91.0% and in full is 94.7%.
  • Lateness is the bigger problem. 62% of the lines that miss OTIF are late only, 34% are short only and 4% are both.
  • No supplier meets the target. The best, Vedara Labs, is at 94.3%.
  • Kestrel Ingredients (61.3%) and Purelon Chemicals (78.7%) place 24% of the lines but cause 51% of the misses.
  • OTIF is improving, from 83.8% in 2024 to 85.7% in 2025 and 89.9% in 2026, but October to December is weaker (82.9%) than the rest of the year (87.0%).
  • 70 lines are open. 10 are overdue, worth EUR 585k. They come from two July orders, from Kestrel and Purelon, 24 and 27 days past their promised dates.

Recommendation

Chase the two overdue July orders from Kestrel and Purelon this week. Agree an improvement plan with both suppliers, then review their OTIF every month. Plan extra lead time for their orders ahead of the October to December peak.

On time = received on or before the promised date. In full = received quantity at least the ordered quantity. OTIF = both, measured per PO line and grouped by promised month. Average days late = days late over late lines only.
